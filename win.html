VICTORY!

The truth about the old days! Nice throwing, bro! But did you know that this used to be really life-threatening? Demonstrating was strictly forbidden back then. If people threw stuff anyway, the government would immediately send in the army. Soldiers would brutally crush the protest, and there were often fatalities. Only since 1983 has demonstrating been a right in the Netherlands. Now you can peacefully express your opinion without the army showing up, man!
